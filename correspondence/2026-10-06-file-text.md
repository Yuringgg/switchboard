## What happens now

Every saved PDF and audio recording is read by the worker and the result kept
on the file's own `attachments` row:

- **PDFs** — their text, read on the worker with `unpdf` (PDF.js). Nothing
  leaves the worker.
- **Recordings** — transcribed by Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`), the
  language detected rather than pinned to English. A 12 MB m4a is minutes long;
  the 84 KB test clip came back in 0.8 s, Tagalog numbers included.
- **Pictures** — not read. Needs a vision model; deferred by Yuri.

**Future files too.** The reader runs right after the file sweep in the same
2-minute loop (`apps/worker/src/index.ts`), on every file whose `text_status`
is null — so a file is read within a couple of minutes of being saved, and the
17 that existed before drain three a pass (about twelve minutes).

The Files page shows a folded **Transcript** / **What it says** under each read
file (the first ~400 characters, `text_preview`). Uriel gets the whole thing
through a new tool, **`read_file`**; every `files` entry now carries a
`fileId` and `readable: true` once it has been read, and `get_files` matches
words inside files as well as their names.

## Follow-up the same evening: the text in pictures (migration 0021)

Yuri asked whether pictures could be read — *"only when it is readable"*. So
pictures get **OCR, not a vision model**: `tesseract.js` on the worker (no
outside service), English model fetched once into `MODEL_CACHE_DIR`. A picture
counts as read only when OCR finds **at least four real words in lines it is
≥70% confident about** (`readableText`); a photo with no readable text is
recorded `empty`, and nothing ever describes what a photo shows.

The 11 saved pictures are three distinct images (one repeated 8 times): an
email-signature card and two bank-transfer receipts. Through the worker's own
`readFileTexts` with real tesseract (database and Azure faked): all three
`done`, 286–323 characters, 3.4 s for the three, ~250 MiB process peak; the
engine starts on the first picture of a pass and is terminated when the pass
ends. PNG / JPEG / WebP / BMP up to 10 MB; GIF and HEIC are not read.

`text_kind` gains `image_text`; the Files page labels it **Text in the
picture**; `read_file` says "The text in the picture." or, for a photo, "There
is no readable text in that picture. Only the text in a picture can be read,
not what it shows." Scanned PDFs are still `empty` (OCR'ing a PDF needs it
rendered to an image first — not done).

**Deployed** as worker revision **0000023** (`42dd6a6`), healthy. Twenty
minutes after 0000022 first ran: 15 of 28 files read, none failed — 6 pictures
(`image_text`, up to 323 characters), 1 PDF, 8 transcripts (up to 13,161
characters); the rest draining three a pass.
